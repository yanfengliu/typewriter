# Devlog

Newest first. One dated line per change that alters behaviour.

- 2026-11-11 — After an independent review (`docs/review-fable-5-2.md`). The line lock is now a part, not a filter on the input: a slotted comb under the key levers that the carriage pushes sideways through a buffer spring, so a locked key (and the space bar) goes down a fifth of its travel and stops; the typist still presses each key asked for, and the letters wait as before; new tour step (17 in all). Backspace moved the carriage two columns near the end of a line or with a firm press (the carriage coasted past the pawl): the pawl is now wedged at the bottom of its stroke. Typist no longer deadlocks when a capital is asked for while the Shift finger is lifting, and lets go of Shift while it waits at a full line (it used to sit on it, so the machine never came to rest). Snapshots hold the impact being tracked, group rest state and contact state. The machine now rests with the carriage on the bell cam. Dry friction can hold a body still. A guided finger stops pressing harder at about 10 N. Status line says "full" at the end of a line instead of "column 66 of 65". 39 tests (was 30): constraint gradients against numerical derivatives, two-body collision, friction, energy ledger, nine snapshot moments, line lock, backspace over columns and forces.
- 2026-11-11 — Ink darkness now `1 − exp(−(F/45 N)²)`: a light touch (1.3 N) prints visibly grey, the default black. Typist gets `reset()` for use after a restore (re-running the Shift tour step stalled because the typist still believed it was holding Shift). Unused shift-lock latch removed. Tour's free-typing step works on the user's own page. `scripts/smoke.sh` checks keys-to-ink in a real browser.
- 2026-11-11 — Key levers drawn with bent-up front ends (view only); screen-reader transcript of the typed page; instruments panel moved bottom left; end-of-page hint.
- 2026-11-11 — Force arrows drawn from solver forces (finger, link tensions, impact, universal bar, mainspring, tooth load, shift rail, hand), on in the tour's linkage, impact and spacing steps. Leaving the tour restores the user's own machine and page. Snapshot restore clears contact flags (a strike begun after restoring from mid-impact lifted off at once; regression test added).
- 2026-11-11 — Guided tour, view modes and interface: 16-step tour that replays the real simulation for each step (side-elevation and slab cross-sections, frozen at the moment the step is about); glass/hidden body, focus, colour by job, exploded, slice, labels, instruments, replay scrubbing, slow motion to 1/500, press-slowly by hand, synthesised sound. Checked by walking every step and the controls in a headless GPU browser at 1920×1080 (175 fps idle, real-time ratio 1.00 while typing).
- 2026-11-11 — 3D model built procedurally from the simulation's own geometry: every part posed from its body, links and springs drawn between live end points, ink stamped from recorded contacts.
- 2026-11-11 — Physics core on the empty `fable_5_2` branch: one-degree-of-freedom bodies with position-projected constraints at 25 µs; type action for 44 keys, universal bar, ribbon lift, escapement with fixed and loose dog, mainspring carriage, shift, space, backspace, line feed, bell, margins; typist queue so no input is lost. 26 tests: solver against closed forms, causal order, rigid closure, energy split, part-removal counterfactuals, timestep convergence, exact replay, typing behaviour.
