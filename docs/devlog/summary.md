# Devlog

Newest first. One dated line per change that alters behaviour.

- 2026-11-11 — Independent review of the trip lock (`docs/review-fable-5-2.md`, Review 2), and its one real finding repaired. The tab went in only if the rocker was within 2 mrad of home; a blow of 25 N or more moves the rocker further than that in one 25 µs substep, so when it arrived in the very substep the carriage passed the trip point neither of the lock's two stops came on, the escapement tripped where it must not, and with the key held down the next line started one column in. The tab is now in whenever the carriage is past the trip point. Only a direct press that hard could do it (the interface's touch stops at 6 N). New test: six blows of 36 to 60 N timed to the substep, and the four substeps either side. The snapshot test gains two trip-lock moments. `docs/physics.md`: the timing windows for two strokes made almost together at the last column are restated by force (they grow with the force of the second stroke); new stated limit: from about 50 N on a key the type sinks so far into the platen, a linear spring, that the other character on its slug prints as well. 46 tests. Checked in a headless browser at 1920×1080: smoke check (text right, no console errors, 0.99 of real time at 230 frames a second; a first run right after the build read 0.82 at 74 frames a second), all 18 tour steps walked, the trip-lock step looked at.
- 2026-11-11 — Trip lock, built at the owner's request after the second review round: a tab under the carriage moves in behind a finger on the dog rocker's shaft as the carriage leaves the last printable column, so nothing can trip the escapement at the end of the line. A key that was already on its way down when the lock comb arrived now stops with its typebar against the universal bar, 11° short of the paper, instead of printing and leaving the next line one column in (0 of 34 timed presses shift the line; it was 8 of 17 and more). If the rocker is still swung out when the tab arrives, the carriage waits for it. Drawn in the model, described in the parts cards, shown in a new tour step (18 in all). Still a limit: a key struck while the carriage is being returned leaves the next line a column in (direct presses only). 45 tests. Checked in a headless browser at 1920×1080: smoke check at 0.99 of real time, the whole tour walked. One walk stalled at step 17 while the test suite and the build ran alongside it; not seen again in two walks run alone, cause not found.
- 2026-11-11 — Second review round, of the repairs below (`docs/review-fable-5-2.md`, round 1). New sheet pressed during a carriage return flung the platen tens of thousands of lines (an old bug): it now only renumbers the platen angle by whole lines, with everything measured from it. The lock comb is no longer thrown when a key held on a tooth drops into its slot as the carriage leaves. The platen may rest wherever its friction holds it. Recorded as limits in `docs/physics.md` §7, not repaired: a key already on its way down can beat the line lock and leave the next line one column in; a backspace begun mid-step can be lost; a key held with a weak steady force lets the carriage step repeatedly. All three need keys pressed directly, which typing never does. A margin stop short of the last tooth was tried against the first and backed out (the escape wheel coasts past it). 42 tests. Checked in a headless browser at 1920×1080: New sheet clicked 25 ms into a return (platen peaked at 11.8 rad/s, the return finished at column 10, typing went on); `scripts/smoke.sh` (text right, no console errors, 0.96 of real time with other jobs loading the machine; it read 0.73 to 0.92 under heavier load earlier the same day); `scripts/walk-tour.sh` (17 steps finish, no console errors).
- 2026-11-11 — After an independent review (`docs/review-fable-5-2.md`). The line lock is now a part, not a filter on the input: a slotted comb under the key levers that the carriage pushes sideways through a buffer spring, so a locked key (and the space bar) goes down a fifth of its travel and stops; the typist still presses each key asked for, and the letters wait as before; new tour step (17 in all). Backspace moved the carriage two columns near the end of a line or with a firm press (the carriage coasted past the pawl): the pawl is now wedged at the bottom of its stroke. Typist no longer deadlocks when a capital is asked for while the Shift finger is lifting, and lets go of Shift while it waits at a full line (it used to sit on it, so the machine never came to rest). Snapshots hold the impact being tracked, group rest state and contact state. The machine now rests with the carriage on the bell cam. Dry friction can hold a body still. A guided finger stops pressing harder at about 10 N. Status line says "full" at the end of a line instead of "column 66 of 65". 39 tests (was 30): constraint gradients against numerical derivatives, two-body collision, friction, energy ledger, nine snapshot moments, line lock, backspace over columns and forces.
