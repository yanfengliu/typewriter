- 2026-11-11 — Independent review of the trip lock (`docs/review-fable-5-2.md`, Review 2), and its one real finding repaired. The tab went in only if the rocker was within 2 mrad of home; a blow of 25 N or more moves the rocker further than that in one 25 µs substep, so when it arrived in the very substep the carriage passed the trip point neither of the lock's two stops came on, the escapement tripped where it must not, and with the key held down the next line started one column in. The tab is now in whenever the carriage is past the trip point. Only a direct press that hard could do it (the interface's touch stops at 6 N). New test: six blows of 36 to 60 N timed to the substep, and the four substeps either side. The snapshot test gains three trip-lock moments. The reviewer then checked the repair (68 replays at the coincident substeps, all right) and caught one wrong sentence about snapshots, now corrected with the third of those moments. `docs/physics.md`: the timing windows for two strokes made almost together at the last column are restated by force (they grow with the force of the second stroke); new stated limit: from about 50 N on a key the type sinks so far into the platen, a linear spring, that the other character on its slug prints as well. 46 tests. Checked in a headless browser at 1920×1080: smoke check (text right, no console errors, 0.99 of real time at 230 frames a second; a first run right after the build read 0.82 at 74 frames a second), all 18 tour steps walked, the trip-lock step looked at.
