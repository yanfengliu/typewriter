## Findings and disposition

- Withdraw review 4's MA-3 recommendation for zero nominal drop. Factory printed 13 gives approximately 1/32 inch (0.79375 mm) depressed-key carriage drop; printed 3 gives 0.265 inch (6.731 mm) character motion. Adopt these common adjustments coherently in the Royal-standard reconstruction, retaining the source/measurement distinction. Their inclusion reopens geometry and clearance checks.
- Remove the unsupported axial series-overload/coil-stop interpretation of link spring 21 and the invented dedicated universal-bar return spring. The clearer ordinary-action figure supports direct rigid-link drive with a separate parallel spring; printed 56 explicitly excludes a spring return for the bar. Exact retainer anchors and its exclusive function remain inferred. The optional silent-key output topology is not substituted for ordinary typing.
- A rigid rod may carry tension while its hook supplies a compressive bearing reaction. Define the actual hook face and separation-positive gap before deciding whether an impulse is attractive. Retain independent follower motion and nonnegative normal reactions.
- Exact zero paper velocity at ink is not established by either manual. A revised printing instrument must locate the actual supported impact in time and material coordinates. An instantaneous impulse model may deposit a localized permanent footprint but cannot claim finite-duration smear prediction. Deleting the stationary gate without a replacement physical check is not acceptable.
